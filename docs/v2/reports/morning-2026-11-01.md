## What changed for users, once merged and deployed

- **Catalog:** the diamond and Si(111) cells are real (D57). `main` and `v2.2` still ship the broken
  ones; #20, not #19, fixes them.
- **Inputs are read as ASE reads them** (D89, D113–D115, D152, D153):
  - CIF symbol case, origin choice and partial occupancy;
  - the PDB `CRYST1 1 1 1` placeholder;
  - atoms closer than 0.5 Å are refused;
  - the preview agrees with the server.
- **No plausible numbers for runs that failed:**
  - NaN results, diverging MD, degenerate cells and malformed parameters are refused with a code
    (D129, D130, D137, D138, D139);
  - convergence is judged honestly (D46).
- **Honest reporting:**
  - devices (ADR-0031);
  - MACE-OFF energy judged relative to the model's isolated atoms (D52);
  - fixed atoms in MD temperature and forces (D131, D132);
  - benchmark labels and units (D97, D107, D108, D156);
  - the accuracy card compares like with like (D90).
- **Security:**
  - local mode binds 127.0.0.1;
  - custom checkpoints are opt-in and loopback-only (D40, D94);
  - visitor limits are trusted only with `MACE_API_TOKEN` (D41, D43);
  - CIF symmetry bombs and chunked uploads are refused early (D133, D142);
  - SMILES conversion is admitted per visitor and time-limited (D136, D151).
- **Demo:** the demo and hero ethanol is a relaxed trans geometry matching CCCBDB (D119). The old
  strained file carries the golden energies as a fixture.
