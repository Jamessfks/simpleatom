## What changed for users, once merged and deployed

- **Catalog:** the diamond and Si(111) cells are real (D57). `main` and `v2.2` still ship the broken
  ones; #20, not #19, fixes them.
- **Inputs are read as ASE reads them** (D89, D113–D115, D152, D153):
  - CIF symbol case, origin choice and partial occupancy;
  - the PDB `CRYST1 1 1 1` placeholder;
  - atoms closer than 0.5 Å are refused;
  - the preview agrees with the server.
- **No plausible numbers for runs that failed:**
  - NaN results, diverging MD, degenerate cells and malformed parameters are refused with a code
    (D129, D130, D137, D138, D139);
  - convergence is judged honestly (D46).
- **Honest reporting:**
  - devices (ADR-0031);
  - MACE-OFF energy judged relative to the model's isolated atoms (D52);
  - fixed atoms in MD temperature and forces (D131, D132);
  - benchmark labels and units (D97, D107, D108, D156);
  - the accuracy card compares like with like (D90).
- **Security:**
  - local mode binds 127.0.0.1;
  - custom checkpoints are opt-in and loopback-only (D40, D94);
  - visitor limits are trusted only with `MACE_API_TOKEN` (D41, D43);
  - CIF symmetry bombs and chunked uploads are refused early (D133, D142);
  - SMILES conversion is admitted per visitor and time-limited (D136, D151).
- **Demo:** the demo and hero ethanol is a relaxed trans geometry (D119). Against CCCBDB's
  experimental geometry, C–C 1.512 Å and C–C–O 108.0° agree, but O–H is 0.013 Å short and H–O–C
  is 3.4° wide. The old strained file carries the golden energies as a fixture.

## `CLAUDE.md` changes

### Rule changes: approved by the owner on 2026-11-01 ("approve and continue"), applied in `c50efba`

1. **MACE-OFF energy check (D52, `da93f38`):**
   - there is no raw E/N band any more;
   - energy is judged relative to the model's own isolated atoms: typical −6 to −0.5,
     enforced −10 to +1 eV/atom;
   - verifier measurements, relative eV/atom: water −3.35, ethanol −3.90, SF6 −0.86,
     CH2Br2 −2.97.
2. **PBE statement (pitfall 3, D85):**
   - the 0.1–0.5 eV/atom band is dropped (no source, and its sign was wrong);
   - added the silicon example: MPA-0 relaxes to 5.467 Å against 5.4309 Å measured (the
     verifier's float64 fit gives 5.4674 Å).
3. **Device policy (pitfall 5, ADR-0031):**
   - the hosted profile refuses `cuda`;
   - a local CUDA fallback warns and records `params.requestedDevice`;
   - CUDA out-of-memory is a coded refusal;
   - `/capabilities` lists only usable devices.
4. **Validator MD heuristics (Automated Validator Checks, D81):**
   - an NVE total-energy excursion above 0.05 eV/atom is an issue;
   - an NVT/NPT second-half mean temperature above 2× the target is an issue.

   Both thresholds are unsourced choices. A run that is too cold is never flagged. `CLAUDE.md`
   now says both things.
5. **"Typical timestep 0.5–2.0 fs" (unchanged, but contradicted).** D130 shows relaxed ethanol
   and water diverging at 2.0 fs, and the engine now warns above 1.5 fs when H is present.

   Applied: 0.5–2.0 fs without hydrogen; 1.0 fs or less with hydrogen for production runs.
   It cites `docs/v2/evidence/2026-11-01/md-divergence`, the H-aware warning above 1.5 fs and
   `md_diverged`. The contract cap stays 4 fs.
